Privacy Policy — Tactics Arena: Wasteland

Effective date: October 17, 2026

1. Data Collection and Local Preferences

Tactics Arena: Wasteland does not collect, sell, or transmit personal identifiable information, geolocation, contacts, or device identifiers to any developer-operated tracking database. The application stores only local user interface preferences in browser storage: tactics-onboarding-v1 (onboarding dismissal) and tactics-locale-v1 (selected interface language), plus session-scoped keys tactics-mwa-auth-v1 and tactics-battle-proof-v1 for temporary wallet authorization and completed battle proof state.

2. Optional Solana Wallet and Public RPC Interactions

Gameplay requires no account or wallet connection. If you voluntarily connect a Solana wallet on Solana Mobile or compatible browsers via Mobile Wallet Adapter, your public wallet address is used locally to:

  • Check Seeker Genesis Token ownership via a public Solana JSON-RPC endpoint (api.mainnet-beta.solana.com).
  • Request an Ed25519 signature over a deterministic battle replay summary when you explicitly press Sign Battle Proof.

Private keys and seed phrases remain exclusively inside your wallet application (such as Seed Vault Wallet, Phantom, or Solflare) and are never accessible to this application.

3. Clearing Local Data

You may clear all local preference and session data at any time by clearing site/app storage in your browser or Android application settings.
